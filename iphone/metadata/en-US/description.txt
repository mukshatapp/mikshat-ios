Mikshat is a maps and navigation app for iPhone and iPad, helping you explore places, plan outdoor trips, and find your destination. It uses OpenStreetMap data and supports offline use of maps you have already downloaded.

Free download and free core features
• Browse maps and search for places and coordinates.
• Navigate by car.
• Use the general compass and find the Qibla direction.
• Copy coordinates and share locations.
• View, manage, delete, and share places you have already saved.
• Record and view tracks.

Annual subscription features
• Direct navigation along a line to your chosen destination.
• Compass guidance toward a point you select.
• Save new places to your favorites.

Subscription expiry does not delete your locally saved places or tracks. Viewing, managing, deleting, and sharing existing saved places remain available.

Subscription details
The subscription lasts one year and renews automatically unless you cancel renewal through your Apple account subscriptions. The app displays the price and currency available to your account from the App Store before you confirm a purchase. Apple processes payment. This plan has no free trial at launch.

Restore your subscription in the app using the Apple account that owns the purchase. Manage or cancel renewal through Apple subscription settings. Access continues until the end of the entitled period unless the purchase is refunded or its eligibility changes. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Purchases, restoration, and verification of renewals require an internet connection. Downloaded maps and supported functions work offline; online services require a connection. Features that use your location need the appropriate permissions. Navigation accuracy can be affected by the location signal, device sensors, and the age of map data.
