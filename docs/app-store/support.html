Mikshat support — iPhone and iPad

Email alhadafsystem@gmail.com for help or privacy requests. Include the device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Mikshat version and steps that led to the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots. Never send your Apple Account password, card details or secret keys; provide coordinates or track files only if needed and you choose to share them.

Restore and manage purchases

Use the Apple Account that owns the purchase, connect to the internet and select Restore Purchases in Mikshat. Pending payments or approval requests must complete before features unlock. Manage or cancel renewal at Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Request refunds through Apple Report a Problem. Read the iOS subscription terms.

Location and local files

Manage location permission and accuracy in device settings. Subscription expiry does not delete locally saved places or tracks. Export important data before removing the app. The iOS privacy policy explains online providers, backups, sharing and privacy requests.
